# Problem–AI Fit: research boundaries

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Distinguish primary material checked here from the local library’s interpretation.

## Baselines and task fit

[Google’s Rules of Machine Learning](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/guides/rules-of-ml) recommends considering a product without ML and establishing metrics. Its early-stage heuristics support a credible baseline; they are not evidence that a fixed fraction of all modern AI proposals should avoid models or that any spreadsheet-expressible output makes ML unnecessary.

## Engine and helper

The [MIT Sloan publication record](https://shop.sloanreview.mit.edu/store/calibrate-ai-use-to-the-decision-at-hand) confirms Pedro Amorim, Amr Saleh, and Ulrika Cederskog Sundling’s article dated 6 May 2026. Its public description distinguishes analytical recommendations from generative assistance around less precise decisions. The six-question scorecard and decomposition are retained from the local article-based skill. Neither the public description nor a favorable score grants an AI authority over consequential actions. “Analytical” does not automatically mean causally valid, and “helper” does not mean harmless.

The original linked an 88% AI-use figure and 39% EBIT-impact figure to the generic McKinsey State of AI page as 2025 survey context. That URL now serves the [25 August 2026 survey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai), reporting 37% attributing some enterprise EBIT impact to AI. Do not let a moving page silently substantiate old numbers or compare survey scopes without checking them. These are respondent reports, not controlled evidence that engine/helper mismatch caused a gap. The durable skill does not need a fixed adoption statistic to make its task-fit distinction.

## Innovation bottlenecks

The [HBR record](https://hbr.org/2026/08/research-the-innovation-problems-ai-cant-solve) confirms De Freitas, Israeli, Nave, Timoshenko, and Toubia’s article dated 14 August 2026. The local material describes ideation, screening, simulated customers, and post-launch feedback. It supplies candidate mechanisms and linked-study leads, not a universal rate at which AI helps or hurts innovation.

Carry the practical distinctions: repetitive model output and human fixation are different effects; presentation quality can confound idea screening; simulated responses need validation against the population and question; synthesis still leaves prioritization unresolved. Do not claim prompting is the only remedy, common-format screening is always beneficial, three of four stages inevitably worsen, or no future model can assist a judgment task. No effect size is asserted here without checking its underlying study.

## Scoping and implementation expectations

The local July 2026 HBR interview with Josh Tyrangiel describes the Cleveland Clinic operating model, clinician product managers, a defined problem and partner, and ongoing stabilization. It is reported experience without a stated comparative sample. The useful questions are what problem is being solved, what good looks like, who owns the result, and what work implementation requires.

It does not show that technology teams should be excluded from pilot ownership, that multiple vendors necessarily prevent scope, or that every domain containing if-then patterns already has usable data and known error rates. Domain and technical competence need to meet in the actual operating model.

## New tasks and worker value

[Acemoglu, Autor, and Johnson’s February 2026 NBER working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34854) distinguishes labor-augmenting, capital-augmenting, automating, expertise-leveling, and new-task-creating change. Its public abstract develops a conceptual account of AI’s effects on worker expertise and incentives. That does not support the prior claim that the taxonomy is useless before labor-market repricing is complete.

A before/after task comparison can test a proposed mechanism in advance and guide later measurement. Task names can hide changes in granularity, responsibility, demand, or expertise. New duties can be valuable work or simply overhead. A deepened existing task may change worker value without establishing new-task creation. Use a consistent unit and measure the claim being made rather than letting a taxonomy label decide AI product fit.

## Novel Insights applied

Revisited the ledger’s task-level measurement discussion, judgment-mode distinctions, and filtering-frame discussion. Capability can differ between tasks in one role; a single-session decision effect is not proof of long-term atrophy; and an assistant selecting evidence can embed a frame even when the human retains final authority.

These support component decomposition, appropriate evaluation, and scrutiny of what the helper includes or leaves out. They do not imply that all search should be automated, all human deliberation preserved unchanged, or all excluded material logged without regard to privacy and cost. Preserve the decision-relevant provenance and limits.
