# Competitive evidence and source limits

Reviewed 13 September 2026.

## Positioning

April Dunford's own [Quickstart Guide to Positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning), 15 March 2021, was read. It connects actual customer alternatives, differentiated capabilities, customer value, best-fit customers, and market category. The article explicitly warns that merely filling a positioning-statement template is not the method. It permits features as differentiated capabilities; the earlier “attributes, not features” distinction was too absolute.

## Agent-mediated shopping

The complete extracted text of the user-provided **Paul F. Accornero, “Algorithmic Shopping Is Here. Is Your Company Ready?”, HBR, 7 August 2026**, was read. It proposes five audit pillars: strategy/leadership, data/technical readiness, commercial readiness, organizational/talent readiness, and governance/ethics.

The article includes anonymous practitioner anecdotes, including a brand ranked third in awareness and seventh in algorithmic selection. Its attribution of that gap entirely to data/API performance is not accompanied by an auditable causal design. Earlier notes saying the article had “no outcome data” were too broad: it has reported anecdotes, but no comparable public dataset establishing the mechanism or a market benchmark. Claims that no company anywhere has published a selection figure cannot be established from this article.

The main skill uses the measurement distinction and dependency questions without treating the article's platform timeline or third-party 2028 forecast as a current verified market measurement. An agent may read structured and unstructured material and use customer brand preferences. Greater platform power, mandatory tolls, and irreversible loss of direct influence are scenarios to investigate, not universal facts.

## Procurement evidence

AICPA's official [SOC services resources](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2) distinguish scoped assurance reports and trust-services criteria. Inspect the actual report and scope; SOC 2 is not a universal AI-safety certification or permission to serve every regulated buyer. Product marketing phrases and planned authorizations are not substitutes for completed evidence and applicable requirements.

## Novel Insights cross-check

Revisited the N/F/G/W/X agentic-commerce discussion around platform dependence, structured data, awareness, and selection. Preserve the distinction between input readiness and actual selection outcomes. Qualify the stronger claims: product data can have value in other channels, brand can enter an agent's inputs, direct-channel switching is not the only possible recourse, and maintenance does not mean no firm can earn an advantage.

The 1–4 capability rubric, three synthesis views, numerical cost examples, and battlecard format are local decision aids. No reviewed source validates an AI-native score cutoff, a universal three-to-six-month capability expiry, a threefold defensibility scale, or a fixed 30–50% trust/switching premium.
