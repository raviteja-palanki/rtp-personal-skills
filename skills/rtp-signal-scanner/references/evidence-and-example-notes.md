# Evidence and example notes

Use these notes when a recommendation relies on the research rather than only the local observations. Check the measure, time period, population, and study version before carrying a number into a new decision.

## Corporate foresight is broader than signal scanning

[Rohrbeck and Kum's 2018 study](https://pure.au.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/179651094/Rohrbeck_Corporate_foresight.pdf) measured future preparedness in 2008 and performance in 2015. It included 70 firms in the profitability analysis and 42 publicly listed firms in the market-capitalization analysis. It reports about 16% profitability for vigilant firms versus a 12% average: roughly 33% higher, not 33 percentage points. Market-cap growth averaged about 75% versus 25%, or 200% more growth relative to the comparison value. This is not a 200% increase in firm value caused by foresight.

Reported deficiency discounts also use different outcomes and comparison groups: roughly 37–44% for profitability and 49–108% for market-cap growth relative to vigilant firms. A negative growth outcome can produce a discount exceeding 100%. Preserve the measure rather than combining them as one general performance penalty.

This is one longitudinal investigation of broader foresight capability. It does not isolate this skill's five-category scanning process or establish that adopting it produces those gains. The practical inference is to connect sensing, interpretation, and decisions while measuring local value.

## ChatGPT referrals: direction, odds, and attribution

[Kaiser and Schulze, Marketing Science 2026](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2025.0489), analyze 973 e-commerce websites with twelve months of data, August 2024–July 2025. The main regression specification uses six months, with other specifications examined for robustness. Channel assignment is last-click, so discovery contributions may be missed. The results are descriptive comparisons, not a randomized test of channel substitution.

The conversion model reports log-odds coefficients of 0.121 for organic search and 0.621 for affiliate relative to ChatGPT. Exponentiation gives odds ratios of about 1.13 and 1.86. These do not mean ChatGPT conversion probability is 13% or 86% lower. Reversing an odds ratio uses its reciprocal; a probability change also depends on the baseline probability. The paper's “likelihood” wording should not erase that distinction.

ChatGPT exceeds paid social in the adjusted overall comparison; high-complexity categories show a different ordering. The [authors' companion site](https://organicllm.org/) explains these differences. The supplied skill's “below every traditional channel” statement was too broad. Do not infer inevitable cannibalization, a guaranteed narrowing gap, or today's performance from the historical panel. Measure the relevant product and customer journey.

## Social listening is not a population census

The supplied skill cites the 2026 *AI in the Wild* report and describes 12,637 use cases drawn from roughly 50,000 records. [Co-author Sara Biuk's announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sarabiuk_annual-report-ai-in-the-wild-2026-activity-7471169442073251841-Qd_r) gives the more precise collection count: **46,067 posts and 12,637 quality-assured use cases**. These are public accounts selected and coded by the study, not a probability sample of all AI users or enterprise work.

The supplied figures—therapy/companionship around 11% versus 5% previously, support as a leading theme, a 16% support-theme share in 2024, and agentic operations and coding entering the top twenty—remain report-specific claims requiring the report's exact categories and comparable methods before reuse. They do not measure time, value, clinical benefit, or population prevalence. A single large deployment and a personal anecdote are not comparable economic units.

The earlier text said six platforms but named only five: Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube. Do not invent the sixth. Verify the actual collection frame before listing it. Reports with similar titles may describe entirely different populations. This source can suggest questions; it should not be a mandatory annual authority for every frontier-product decision.

## Other research connections and their limits

- **B2B buying:** the supplied Joshi/Buche/Schwaer June 2026 HBR account reports more than 80% first-party source content in a Digitas/Publicis fintech pilot and an IDC forecast of 62% AI-led B2B demand generation by 2028. A pilot's citation mix and an analyst forecast have different meanings. Neither establishes a universal source budget or the binary rule that selection and synthesis require unrelated tactics. `marketing-to-ai-agents` carries the fuller diagnostic.
- **Scharmer's three intelligences:** pattern prediction, perspective-taking, and field sensing form a conceptual framework for broadening attention. Its philosophical lineage does not establish a measured performance effect or prove that records cannot contain emerging signals. Avoid importing unrelated cognitive-debt percentages from the ledger; the earlier neuroscience figure remains disputed and is not evidence for this scanning design.
- **Reported versus lived organization:** the supplied practitioner anecdote motivates triangulating dashboards with work and people's experiences. It does not establish that distortion rises monotonically with management layers, that AI summaries always worsen it, or that only unannounced conversations reveal the truth.
- **Automated detection:** practitioner and vendor accounts suggest useful collection and triage tools. They do not demonstrate that every automated scanner is earlier, cheaper, or sufficiently accurate. Include reviewer work and missed signals in the comparison.
- **Opening quotation:** the earlier sentence attributed to Andy Grove about danger when everything looks fine was not traceable in this revision. The main skill retains attention to complacency without presenting that wording as a verified quotation.

## Treat numeric scenarios as examples, not facts

The supplied examples include support themes rising 20% weekly or 30–40% monthly; ten to fifteen hires; hiring time dropping from ninety to thirty days or by 60%; fifteen category entrants; ten acquisitions in a year; two or three similar customer requests; and a budget timetable moving by three months. These are illustrative prompts for investigation. They are not universal thresholds, observed industry statistics, or evidence of one inevitable cause.

The “80% of unicorn IPOs are now from Asia” example had no supporting source or defined population. Do not repeat it as a market fact. An illustrative regional shift should use clearly hypothetical data or verified current evidence.

The underlying examples remain useful: integration-related churn may reveal an unmet need, fewer basic tiers may indicate a segment change, a cloud partnership may extend distribution, and shifts in compliance questions may reveal procurement requirements. Confirm the cause and scope before acting. A bill reaching a hearing differs from an enacted requirement, and a buyer's RFP criterion differs from law.

## Novel Insights applied to the scan

The ledger's shared-source notes show why several articles can amount to one evidential input. Track the underlying source, sample, and method. Publication in the same venue is a reason to inspect dependence, not proof that authors cannot provide independent evidence.

The declaration-versus-use distinction helps interpret job posts, executive statements, patents, and surveys. These can be useful signals, but none is identical to observed deployment. The instrument-comparison insight from the Ferriss discussion asks whether conflicting evidence concerns the same population, task, and fidelity. It does not justify dismissing inconvenient evidence merely because your private interpretation feels better.

Finally, include the cost of inaction. A justified decision to retain the plan remains a decision; it should have a rationale and an appropriate review trigger. Counting only changes would make a scanning program look productive when it merely causes churn.
