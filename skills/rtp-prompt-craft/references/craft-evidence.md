The [researchers' Innovation Growth Lab account, 4 December 2025](https://www.innovationgrowthlab.org/blog/domain-experts-are-essential-for-creativity-in-the-algorithmic-age) identifies Lazar, Lifshitz-Assaf, Ayoubi, and Emuna's published **Academy of Management Journal 68(5), 2025** article, [DOI 10.5465/amj.2023.1307](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2023.1307). Reported semantic clusters were one for novices using standard search, two for experts using standard search, two for novices using exploratory search, and five for experts using exploratory search. ## Arithmetic and interpretation

- A 30-case 20/60/20 split is 6 happy-path, 18 edge, and 6 adversarial cases. Ten per group is a different design.
- Equal $0.01 attempts and 60 verified successes out of 100 imply $1/60 = $0.0167 per success. This does not treat all accepted outputs as correct.
- At a hypothetical $3/million input tokens, 250 million input tokens cost $750; 50 million cost $150. The $600 daily difference totals $18,000 over 30 days, before other costs and cache effects.
- A perfect observed safety score applies to the tested cases and conditions. It cannot establish that all future attacks will fail.
