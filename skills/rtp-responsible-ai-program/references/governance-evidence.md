# Governance evidence and interpretation

Editorial check: 13 September 2026. The main skill, its concept guide, and the complete locally supplied SHARP article were read. The other sources below were checked at the stated level, not treated as independent audits of organizations.

## SHARP source and adaptations

Işık and Goswami, [The Three Obstacles Slowing Responsible AI](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/the-three-obstacles-slowing-responsible-ai/), online 28 October 2025, Winter 2026 issue, reprint 67205, draws on over twenty interviews. The primary local PDF explicitly describes SHARP as non-linear and not a universal template. It includes embedded project ownership, a co-developed healthcare checklist, enterprise-risk translation, recognition/performance practices, and ethics labs/retrospectives. The source offers reported practice and qualitative interpretation, not validated four-question diagnostic scoring, a ten-point maturity model, or the later twelve-mechanism catalog.

The source's concern about diminished ethical reflection is a research-informed warning, not a longitudinal effect measured by these interviews. Training, discussion, committees, and checklists can contribute when they are connected to working decisions; the source does not justify dismissing them wholesale.

## Enforcement examples do not prove disclosure rewards

[The FTC's Amazon case record](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/amazoncom-inc-rosca-ftc-v-timeline-item-2025-09-25) concerns Prime enrollment/cancellation practices. Its September 2025 $2.5 billion settlement comprises a $1 billion civil penalty and $1.5 billion in refunds. This is not an AI safety study or evidence that proactive AI disclosure caused leniency.

[The Apitor order](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/Apitor-Order.pdf) enters a $500,000 civil penalty and suspends it based on financial representations. [The FTC case account](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/apitor) concerns children's geolocation collection through a third-party SDK. Suspension because of inability to pay does not establish a reward for early disclosure. Neither case proves that customers never value safety or that disclosure improves regulatory latitude in every setting.

The original CDR “registry → design-time oversight → external signal” sequence is a useful organizing idea. The “Sacrifice” quadrant and opposite-audience communication hypothesis remain practitioner interpretations, not a measured universal rule. Keep truthful, relevant disclosure and assess commercial effects separately.

## Legal references

[CFPB's withdrawn-guidance list](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/guidance/withdrawn-guidance/) records Circular 2023-03 as withdrawn on 12 May 2025. Do not cite it as current guidance. Withdrawal alone does not repeal underlying legal obligations. For adverse-action explanations, determine the applicable current requirements and decision evidence.

[EU AI Act Article 14](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02024R1689-20260727) addresses human oversight for covered high-risk systems, including appropriate intervention capabilities. Establish system classification, organizational role, applicable timing, and current consolidated text before giving a specific compliance prescription. The Act does not make a particular chief-officer title, reporting line, or logged explanation click the universal test of compliance. Other privacy, employment, consumer-protection, and sector rules need their own applicability assessment.

## Historical research leads and their limits

- **German bank, 2019–2025:** the supplied account describes one firm's lending officers being answerable without override and giving explanations that did not reflect decision factors. The source does not establish that all four diagnostic questions were answered yes, a universal seniority gradient in fabrication, or that two generated explanations form ground truth. Check actual decision evidence and legitimate escalation.
- **Joseph Wallace/Adobe, June 2026:** a practitioner governance argument supplies the three authority questions and federated example. No measured intervention outcomes are supplied. Use the questions on their merits; no claim that all ethics roles are merely advisory follows.
- **Minimum viable governance:** agility, trustworthy design, integration, and opportunity sensitivity can coexist with strong controls. The library's two-quarter-collapse and invisible-control interpretations are hypotheses. The relevant original measures do not independently validate them.
- **272-expert risk elicitation:** it can highlight cross-organization exposure and responsibility. It does not establish that switching providers is a deployer's only control or that internal governance cannot limit a rented model's use.
- **54-person cognitive preprint:** the original podcast lead does not establish a trade-off between reviewer independence and competence in organizational governance. Build and test a review arrangement that has both.
- **Chan/Rand, n=2,512:** the original study lead concerns willingness to inspect AI reasoning under particular incentives. Do not extrapolate to every credit, medical, hiring, or judicial review, or equate opening an explanation with understanding it. Mandatory engagement is a possible intervention to test, not a general legal inference.
- **MIT CISR, n=132:** company disclosures about agent accountability capabilities are not proof of actual human-review quality. The 95%/sixty-second screening rule is the library's proposal, not a validated result of that survey.
- **Kiro incident:** the historical thirteen-hour outage/credential account remains a reported case, not an independently verified causal analysis here. The technical prescription should inspect effective privilege and approval enforcement, not assert that a distinct machine identity is the only solution.
- **Credit Suisse example:** the original incorrectly names “João Horta-Osório” as CEO. The company's [January 2022 registration-document supplement](https://derivative.credit-suisse.com/fx/fr/en/document/get/id/E75776FF-7BA2-4C41-874A-AC7456AC3F7F) identifies **António Horta-Osório as chairman**, succeeded by Axel Lehmann. The behavioral claim that the event instantly destroyed every subordinate's compliance beliefs is not measured evidence.
- **Second-order committee work:** the April 2026 HBR argument supplies illustrative mechanisms, not a demonstrated inevitability that automation ends deliberation. Review whether criteria remain owned, examined, and revisable.

## Numerical and conceptual boundaries

The original score bands, one/twenty-four-hour escalation examples, 5/20/5 discussion, monthly/quarterly/annual practice, twelve-month/eight-quarter review windows, and ninety-day plan are local planning aids. They are not SHARP-validated thresholds.

Novel Insights preserves important connections among authority, resources, detection, participation, and criteria maintenance. Later entries also challenge the artifact-modification proxy and identify role deletion. Read those qualifications alongside the initial hypotheses. A governance review should be able to conclude that a standard artifact, unchanged criterion, justified approval, or embedded preventive control is working when the evidence supports it.
