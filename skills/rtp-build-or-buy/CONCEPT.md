# Build or Buy: reading guide

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for the current decision procedure. It separates the capability being obtained, the components owned, the hosting route, and the ongoing responsibilities.

The six checks assess task fit, simpler baselines, data, latency, economics, and maintenance. Apply hard requirements to each candidate; compare the options that remain eligible. Prompting, retrieval, tuning, and a vendor product can be combined.

The [ownership reference](references/ownership-and-provider-boundaries.md) develops the nine companion lenses and provider-data questions. The [evidence and calculation reference](references/evidence-and-calculations.md) preserves useful historical examples with corrected assumptions.

The earlier long concept guide remains historical material in `archive/CONCEPT-pre18JUL2026.md` in the source library. Its older approach descriptions and numerical assumptions are superseded by the current guidance.
