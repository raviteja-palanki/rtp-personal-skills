# Evidence and historical claims

This reference keeps the earlier skill's research connections and examples available without turning them into universal investment rules. A company example, a practitioner thesis, an empirical study, and an independent replication carry different kinds of evidence. Source prestige alone does not determine the strength of a claim.

## Numerical rules that should not decide the assessment

The earlier skill used these numbers as directional guidance without adequate support for general application:

- 5–17% of AI products surviving eighteen months; survivors needing three or more compounding moats.
- A total of at least 12/25 meaning defensible, 8–11 meaning thin, and below 8 meaning exposed; fewer than three scores of at least 3 meaning fragile regardless of the total.
- Replication in one or two quarters, about a year, or several years as universal score anchors.
- Data advantages lasting 12–24 months, workflow 18–36 months, harness 24+ months, and trust 5–10 years; standards collapsing workflow advantage in six months; model-dependent advantages expiring in 6–12 months.
- A roughly 40% vertical-product advantage, an eight-versus-sixteen-week build comparison producing a forty-week compounding lead, and stale data producing about 35% more hallucinations.

Preserve the questions these heuristics raised—replicability, timing, focus, and freshness—but use measured conditions and scenarios. A slower initial build does not mathematically create a forty-week lead at the same future date. A single strong barrier can matter; three correlated claims are not three independent defenses. Trust loss is not an automatic binary reset.

The earlier cold-start timebox of 30–45 minutes is a facilitation option. It does not make missing evidence sufficient for a consequential decision. An eighteen-month scenario can help when relevant; it is not a universal survival test.

## Model capability and technical differentiation

The [MIT FutureTech study by Mertens, Fischl-Lanzoni, and Thompson](https://futuretech.mit.edu/publication/is-there-secret-sauce-in-large-language-model-development), dated 6 February 2026, reports developer-specific efficiency differences in a sample of 809 models released from 2022–2025. It also finds a substantial role for training compute at the frontier. This is inconsistent with treating every model and technical method as identical. It does not establish the duration or profitability of any particular company's moat.

The useful application-level question remains: what advantage persists if the customer or competitor can access a different strong model? Some data, routines, and interfaces transfer; some require rebuilding. “Model-agnostic” is an aspiration to test, not a guarantee that any model can replace any other without changing outcomes.

The older concept guide placed Constitutional AI entirely inside context engineering and implied it was an automatic user-data flywheel. [Anthropic's original research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback) describes supervised learning and reinforcement learning with AI feedback guided by principles. It is not simply a system prompt or proof that each customer interaction updates a model. Published research also does not establish that the company has no exclusive data, distribution, or workflow advantages.

## Economics and complementary resources

**Benartzi, Long, and Puntoni, HBR, 1 June 2026.** The supplied skill reports a wealth-management valuation illustration in which 5% rather than 3% growth increases estimated value by roughly 50%, and a senior-executive roundtable's roughly 135% AI premium, or 2.35× valuation. These are model- or judgment-specific claims, not audited outcomes or general valuation multipliers. The associated “half the cost base × 10% reduction = 5% total cost reduction” arithmetic does not establish a 10% ceiling on firm-value improvement. Assess cash flows, margins, persistence, investment, and risk for the actual decision.

**BCG economists, HBR, July 2026.** The supplied examples of U.S. auto margins falling from roughly 30% and food spending declining from roughly 40% to 13% of household budgets are historical illustrations whose definitions and periods were not verified in this revision. They cannot establish a law that renters never retain savings or that all surplus goes to the scarcest non-falling input.

**Writing Code versus Shipping Code.** The local research note attributes 17.3× code output and 1.3× product releases to Demirer, Musolff, and Yang, NBER working paper 35275, May 2026. It explicitly leaves the denominator and counterfactual unresolved. The primary page and PDF returned access errors during this revision. Treat the numbers as **unverified for reuse** until the actual estimand and design are read. Dividing two different outcome multipliers does not directly measure the percentage of economic value lost, and a modeled counterfactual is not intrinsically invalid; its assumptions need examination. The useful question is how a local acceleration affects end-to-end delivery.

**MIT Sloan CISR, September 2025, 132 enterprises.** The supplied digital-colleague survey connects AI capability with enterprise rules and data. It does not isolate those assets' causal contribution to margin or demonstrate that such organizational resources cannot be rented or replicated. Similarly, concentrated compute supply is a market condition to investigate, not proof that agent adoption can only centralize control.

The Journal of Marketing article linked in the prior skill ([DOI 10.1177/00222429251367342](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222429251367342)) was used to suggest that visible data collection can affect trust. Its specific moderator, population, and estimates require the actual study before numerical reuse. Data benefit and trust can reinforce or oppose one another depending on the practice and customer expectations.

**Metaphysic AI, HBR Cold Call, August 2026.** The supplied discussion contrasts access arrangements for creative work and offers no deal terms or production figures. Consent and payment arrangements can sustain valuable participation. The case does not show that every unpaid or nonexclusive flow is static, that a platform cannot acquire new lawful inputs by another route, or that only gated inputs and hidden outputs support defensibility. Output imitation is one constraint alongside contracts, execution, distribution, and service.

**The Atlantic/OpenAI licensing discussion.** The supplied salary claim of two to three times industry pay and the causal story linking it to licensing proceeds were not supported by disclosed deal terms. Do not repeat them as established facts. A continuing production relationship and a historical archive can have different value without one becoming worthless or model collapse guaranteeing renewal revenue.

**Dan Wang on process knowledge.** Tacit and distributed know-how can be difficult to transfer. It is not necessarily impossible to buy access to it through hiring, acquisition, training, or collaboration. Evaluate transfer channels and demonstrated competence. Do not attribute a company's decline to lost process knowledge from an anecdote or treat observation hours as the asset itself.

**Expertise and retrieval, MIT SMR research highlight, August 2026.** The supplied table reports novice/expert solution-cluster counts of 1/2 under standard search and 2/5 under exploratory retrieval. It does not show equal cluster counts under standard search. A reported null on another outcome is a different result. The sample size of 245 does not alone establish that the study was underpowered; effect sizes, design, variance, and clustering matter. Use the case to motivate a local tool-and-expertise comparison, not to conclude that default search always makes experts perform like novices. More semantic clusters do not necessarily mean better solutions.

**Employee-held expertise.** The supplied Acemoglu/Autor/Johnson taxonomy was reported without a primary-source verification in that pass. Its useful prompt is to distinguish expertise held by people from the firm's documented assets. People can leave, while teams may also preserve capability through routines, redundancy, and successful transfer. This is an empirical dependency question, not proof that expertise cannot support a firm-level moat.

## Trust, extension, disclosure, and learning pace

**Porsche, HBR Cold Call, March 2025.** The supplied case describes maintaining a recognizable performance promise through expansion into SUVs, other price points, and EVs. FY2024 deliveries were reported as 310,718, compared with roughly 10,000 vehicles annually in the early 1990s. This is a historical illustration, not a controlled test of brand-extension causation or proof that every product clears an identical lap-time threshold. For an AI extension, define the comparable customer promise and an appropriate test for the new task.

**Speedskating, HBS case 725-391 discussed in February 2026.** The supplied story contrasts a visible pushing technique with the difficulty of executing it at an elite level. It also describes the disclosure decision before the 2022 Games. Do not infer that one program exclusively possessed execution quality for every intervening year merely from selected results. The transferable questions are what rivals can observe, what they can reproduce, what they already know, and what disclosure enables. Date-specific competitive outcomes need verification before external reuse.

**Shopify and autonomous iteration.** The supplied 53% rendering improvement over 93 autonomous commits, described as five human-years of work, was not located in a suitable primary source during this revision. Keep it out of a quantified business case until the metric, task, baseline, time period, and contribution are established. Commit count cannot be converted into person-years without evidence. The retained loop is hypothesis → controlled test → evaluation → permitted release → monitoring.

**Apple, Anthropic, Google Maps, Weather.com, Slack, Salesforce, and other earlier examples.** Use them as prompts to inspect data, integration, reputation, scale, or participation effects, not as pre-scored companies. Apple Intelligence's integration and privacy architecture do not show that every iOS user supplies shared training data or that four moats are proven. Google Maps' history does not imply every competitor starts from zero. A Weather.com dataset is not uniquely useful merely because it is large. Slack can have network effects as well as switching costs; a network need not be two-sided. The concept guide's fixed 200-hour learning cost, 1,000× incumbent advantage, and hypothetical GPT-7 superiority figures were illustrations without a validated basis.

## Provider reuse, shared signals, and acquisition

**William Marks, HBR, 8 September 2026.** The article proposes employee feedback, synthetic reuse, and broader capability learning as possible leakage channels. The last is explicitly a hypothesis without a cited incident in the supplied note. The original sandbox quotation should not be read as a technical law; it depends on which service is contacted and what information leaves the boundary.

As checked on 13 September 2026, [Anthropic's commercial terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms), displaying an effective date of 17 June 2025, distinguish customer content from voluntary feedback and prohibit model training on customer content under those terms. Its [training FAQ](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training) describes the commercial default and circumstances involving feedback or permission. This does not establish identical OpenAI terms, treat every correction or interaction log as unrestricted feedback, or prove actual competitive leakage. Check the route, feature, configuration, agreement, and observed use. `build-or-buy` carries the detailed boundary check.

**Algorithmic pricing.** [Assad, Clark, Ershov, and Xu, Journal of Political Economy 2024](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10187765/) study German gasoline markets using inferred adoption timing and an instrumental-variable design. The primary abstract reports margin effects depending on local competition and whether all stations adopt. The supplied skill's approximately 28% duopoly effect is context-specific and requires the exact comparison from the paper. It does not prove that every firm used an identical algorithm, or that public inputs necessarily force lower margins. Treat this as counterevidence to a universal convergence claim, not a recommendation to coordinate pricing.

**AI financial advice and brand presence.** The supplied Choukhmane et al. working-paper account describes roughly 1,000 U.S. adults and unprompted Vanguard/iShares mentions of about 6%/3.4%, versus fewer than 0.4% of prompts naming either. With an upper bound rather than the exact prompt denominator, the quoted 15×/8× multipliers are not exact estimates. A randomized label treatment does not necessarily randomize brand exposure or identify its cause. Corpus presence, implicit user context, retrieval, and model behavior need to be distinguished before claiming causal distribution value.

**Acquisition research.** The supplied Srivastava et al. June 2026 HBR account describes roughly 17,000 public-company deals over fifteen years and no reliable association between bought innovation and subsequent growth. The full identification and outcome definitions were not checked here. That does not establish that assets, talent, routines, or innovation never transfer. The assessment should ask which transfers succeed, at what cost, and what scarce equivalents rivals could buy.

**Startup compression and open-source distribution.** The supplied venture-portfolio figures—roughly $2 million to Series A, around 80% less capital, and 20–40% less time—are self-interested, unverified claims for that cohort, not a competitive clock for every incumbent. CUDA, Android, and an older Grok Build reference illustrate ecosystem questions; they do not establish identical openness, licensing, economics, or outcomes. Check the actual project before using it as a current case.

## How the Novel Insights qualify the framework

Reread K, N, W, the stock/flow development, the provider-learning join, and their later qualifications. They identify useful tests of value, capture, exposure, and ownership. Their older absolute language—renters never retain savings, an archive cannot earn recurring value, public representation earns no margin, or portability defeats exclusivity—goes beyond the evidence. Keep the proposed mechanism and test it under the actual market and contractual conditions.

Finding another article repeating the same case does not supply independent confirmation. When a claim is unresolved, record what evidence would settle it and keep the recommendation proportionate to what is known.
