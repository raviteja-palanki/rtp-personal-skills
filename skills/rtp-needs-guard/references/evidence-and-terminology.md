# Needs Guard: evidence and terminology

Reviewed 13 September 2026.

## Established theory and local applications

[Self-Determination Theory’s basic-needs overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/topics/application-basic-psychological-needs/) distinguishes autonomy, competence, and relatedness and distinguishes need frustration from mere absence of satisfaction. Autonomy concerns volition, not unrestricted independence. Relatedness concerns human connection and care. These concepts help investigate work experience; they do not establish a fixed AI-adoption curve or justify diagnosing a worker from behavior alone.

The [HBR publication record](https://hbr.org/2026/03/why-gen-ai-feels-so-threatening-to-workers) identifies Hermann, Puntoni, and Morewedge’s article in the March–April 2026 issue. The Acknowledge–Watch–Align–Redesign–Empower response and coping discussion are retained from the local article-based material. The separate Autonomy–Work identity–Affiliation–Routine/expertise–Expertise recognition lenses are a local diagnostic aid. Do not attribute that five-lens score to SDT or present it as the article’s validated instrument.

Training, role redesign, better software, and truthful organizational decisions can each affect needs. The older absolute that no training or change-management intervention can help was too broad. A staffing threat is an actual organizational issue as well as a possible psychological one; supportive language alone does not resolve it.

## Survey numbers and adoption predictions

The previous claim that approximately 31% actively resist AI was not verified here. An attempted Gartner primary-site check was blocked, so that figure does not support a rate or causal claim in this skill.

[BCG’s AI at Work 2025 report](https://web-assets.bcg.com/fd/0d/bcc5dfae4cbaa08c718b95b16cf5/ai-at-work-2025-slideshow-june-2025-edit-02.pdf), checked during the related Attitudinal Segmentation revision, reports 54% of respondents **would** use AI without company authorization. That is stated willingness in the survey, not observed unauthorized use and not evidence of which need caused a behavior. Preserve the verb and population if reusing it.

No evidence reviewed validates the old predictions that five Green ratings produce sustained adoption, two lead to shadow AI, or zero to one produce active resistance. Month-three dips, month-six decay, 60% adoption as a falsifier, ten-to-fifteen interview counts, and two-to-three-week intervention schedules are not universal thresholds. AWARE colors should summarize evidence and uncertainty, not manufacture precision.

## Novel Insights applied

The ledger’s cost-side adoption discussion highlights effort that disappears from the official time-saving story. Its later work asks who receives saved time and distinguishes participation in a rollout from control over the benefit. Use these as questions to investigate rather than treating an anecdote as proof of every non-user’s motive. Several claims share a single case, so they are not independent corroboration.

The guide’s control-job distinction also matters: explanation is not verified reasoning, an override button is not effective authority without time and competence, and a new role description is not evidence that old work disappeared. The revision therefore asks for lived experience, task outcomes, and actual resource changes alongside the needs framework.
