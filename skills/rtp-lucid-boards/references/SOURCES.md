## Sources checked for this revision

- [Lucid’s diagram guidance](https://lucid.co/blog/wow-worthy-lucidchart-diagrams) supports clarity, consistency, contrast, spacing, alignment, and limited font variation. Its current text does not itself state a three-color cap.
- [Lucid’s flowchart guide](https://lucid.co/diagram/flowchart/how-to-make-a-flowchart) does suggest no more than three colors in a flowchart. Applying a small palette to classroom posters is this skill’s design choice, not a universal platform limit.
- [C4 notation guidance](https://c4model.com/diagrams/notation) supports clear scope, understandable notation, a key, and care with grayscale and color vision. It does not require every diagram to fit one A4 sheet or forbid other visual forms.
- [Duarte’s Glance Test](https://www.duarte.com/resources/guides-tools/the-glance-test/) offers a three-second comprehension exercise and advice on focus, whitespace, and contrast. It is a design exercise, not a universal measured reading threshold.
- [Lucid Standard Import reference](https://lucid.readme.io/v1.0/docs/reference-si) describes formatted text and automatic scaling when font size is omitted. Its relevant text was accessible through indexed primary-page content although direct page extraction did not expose that section consistently.
- [Import overview](https://lucid.readme.io/docs/overview-si) explains the package and warns that rendering can change over time. [Custom-shape text areas](https://lucid.readme.io/docs/textareas) distinguish point and pixel units in their own schema; do not transfer those fields blindly to another API.
- [Lucid export help](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16324571257492-Export-or-print-a-Lucid-document), updated September 10, 2026, documents downloadable PDF, PNG, JPEG, and SVG exports. It corrects the old implication that one connector’s PNG-only limitation describes the product.
- [W3C font-size specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-fonts-4/#font-size-prop) explains font scaling and why visible glyphs need not fill the em box. The skill’s print-size examples are calculations, not tests on a physical TAPMI handout.
- [W3C contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) is the reference for the familiar digital text thresholds. The six main-skill color ratios were recalculated from sRGB relative luminance. Digital ratios do not certify projector or photocopier output.

The local TAPMI rasterizer and S01 trace note were also read. The renderer uses Pillow, pixel-sized fonts, role defaults, and a limited set of shapes. A reconstructed PNG cannot certify the live Lucid rendering without comparison. No existing classroom board was recreated or tested during this skill edit.

12. **Mark Harrower and Cynthia A. Brewer, ColorBrewer.** Retained attribution from the original register. 13. **Bang Wong, “Points of view: Color blindness,” Nature Methods 8, 441 (2011).** Retained bibliographic attribution for the Okabe–Ito discussion. 14. **Viewing-distance heuristic.** The original log gives roughly one inch of letter height per ten feet without a traceable primary study. Retain only as a historical heuristic, not a measured standard. Point size is not the same as visible letter height, and actual viewing conditions control the test.
