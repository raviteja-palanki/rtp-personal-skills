## Lucid, as Lucid and its users write it

1. Lucid Software. *Tips to create wow-worthy Lucidchart diagrams.* https://lucid.co/blog/wow-worthy-lucidchart-diagrams Clarity, consistency, contrast. About three colors on a flowchart. One or two fonts. Align. Space. No overlapping lines. This is a flowchart floor. TAPMI posters steal the cap, not the arrows.
2. Lucid Software. *How to Make a Flowchart.* https://lucid.co/diagram/flowchart/how-to-make-a-flowchart "Try to use no more than three colors in a single flowchart." Same cap as (1).
3. Lucid Community. *Before/After: Aesthetic diagramming tips.* 13 Jul 2023. https://community.lucid.co/inspiration-5/before-after-aesthetic-diagramming-tips-593 Three or four colors. Too much color overstimulates. Curved lines vs swimlane confusion.
4. Zoe Heimdal (Salesforce documentation designer). *Is anybody doing more designerly diagrams in Lucidchart?* Lucid Community, 15 Jan 2026. https://community.lucid.co/inspiration-5/is-anybody-doing-more-designerly-diagrams-in-lucidchart-12593 Lucid defaults are utilitarian. World-class Lucid is branded information design. Grid. Simplify complexity. Contrast scan. Do not rely on color alone. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/zoeheimdal No public X account found 03 SEP 2026.

## Diagram grain (not Lucid-native, imported on purpose)

9. Brown, Simon. C4 model notation. One zoom level per diagram. Title names the diagram. A color you cannot explain gets a key or gets removed. Watch black-and-white printers. https://c4model.com/diagrams/notation
10. Brown, Simon. *Visio, draw.io, LucidChart, Gliffy, etc - not recommended for software architecture diagrams.* DEV Community, 4 Jul 2020. https://dev.to/simonbrown/visio-draw-io-lucidchart-gliffy-etc-not-recommended-for-software-architecture-diagrams-4bmm Names Lucidchart in the drawing-tool bucket. TAPMI posters are posters, not architecture models. Steal the zoom rule. Do not apply the ban to a classroom wall.
11. Duarte, Nancy. Glance Test. One idea per page. Signal vs noise. White space isolates meaning. https://www.duarte.com/resources/guides-tools/the-glance-test/ Also HBR, "Do Your Slides Pass the Glance Test?," 22 Oct 2012. No Lucid-specific Duarte tweet opened.

## Print color

12. Harrower, Mark, and Cynthia A. Brewer. ColorBrewer. Qualitative palettes collapse in grayscale when lightness is equal.
13. Wong, Bang. "Points of view: Color blindness." *Nature Methods* 8, 441 (2011). Okabe-Ito palette: `#E69F00` `#56B4E9` `#009E73` `#F0E442` `#0072B2` `#D55E00` `#CC79A7` `#000000`. Never white on `#F0E442`.
14. Print viewing-distance rule of thumb: about one inch of letter height per ten feet. 72 pt is one inch. Use as a TAPMI back-row check, not as a measured lab result.
