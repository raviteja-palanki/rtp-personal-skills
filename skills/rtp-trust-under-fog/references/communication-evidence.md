# Communication under uncertainty: cases and limits

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Cases inform judgment; they do not establish universal communication laws.

## Wells Fargo

The [board’s 10 April 2017 investigation summary](https://newsroom.wf.com/news-releases/news-details/2017/Wells-Fargo-Board-Releases-Findings-of-Independent-Investigation-of-Retail-Banking-Sales-Practices-and-Related-Matters/default.aspx) identifies sales culture, performance management, decentralized oversight, leadership resistance, and minimization of problems. The full 110-page investigation was not read in this pass. This supports examining how promises, targets, incentives, and contrary evidence interact; it does not isolate communication as the cause of the scandal.

The earlier “5% growth instead of 10% would have avoided fake accounts” example was a speculative counterfactual, not an observed alternative. The quoted recovery statement attributed to new leadership had no verified source and should not be presented as an actual quotation.

The [Federal Reserve’s 3 June 2025 announcement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20250603a.htm) removed the asset-growth restriction imposed in 2018 after specified governance and risk-management conditions were met. Other provisions remained at that announcement. This is a dated regulatory milestone, not a measurement of complete trust recovery or a five-year recovery rule.

## Porsche and CBA disclosure cases

Targeted passages in the user-provided **The Evolution of Luxury Brand Porsche**, HBR Cold Call, **4 March 2025**, were read. Stefan Thomke describes roughly 150 internal milestones with eleven shown to buyers and a classroom debate about transparency, including delayed shipping and a ship loss. These are case-author descriptions. The interview does not establish that all progress photos reduce anxiety or that affected customers have no useful recourse.

Targeted passages in **Why the Commonwealth Bank of Australia Opened Up to Customers about Credit Card Risks**, HBR Cold Call, **March 2026**, were read. Leslie John discusses the asymmetric salience of disclosure’s costs and benefits. The focal experiment’s outcome is withheld in the episode. The underlying **Commonwealth Bank of Australia: Unbanklike Experimentation**, Buell and John, is a 2018 teaching case; its publication date is not the date of a new 2026 experiment. [Publisher case record](https://store.hbr.org/product/commonwealth-bank-of-australia-unbanklike-experimentation/619018).

The Novel Insights comparison near lines 2489–2509 combines actionability, timing, and asymmetric feedback. These are hypotheses for communication design. It is too strong to conclude that all well-instrumented teams under-disclose, that absence of recourse justifies silence, or that the case proves explanation affects uptake but cannot improve safety. Audit the actual decisions, measurements, obligations, and options.

## Storoni and arousal

Targeted passages in the user-provided **Redefining What Efficiency Means in the Age of AI** were read, including the introductory framing and discussion of apprehension, norepinephrine, and learning. The **13 May 2026 HBR On Leadership** item replays an IdeaCast conversation with Mithu Storoni; it is not a new controlled leadership-communication experiment.

The interview offers a mechanism and practitioner advice. It provides no measured population establishing that a calm room learns nothing, panic eliminates all learning, silence means disengagement, or leaders should maintain everyone at an upper edge of apprehension. Individual differences, task complexity, prior knowledge, and context matter. Use clear information, support, meaningful participation, and comprehension checks rather than emotional-state targets.

## Numerical and logical corrections

- A 99% result on a large test set does not guarantee future classification. Valid-label enforcement is distinct from correct categorization.
- In a 60–85% automation scenario, manual handling is 15–40%; the lower automation case needs more manual capacity. A 50% result falls outside that range.
- A two-week review can establish what is known then; it cannot guarantee sufficient evidence for every question.
- Board, customer, frontline, and regulator uncertainty percentages in the old skill had no defined denominator or empirical basis.
- Accuracy, confidence, adoption, coverage, and cost reduction are different measures. State the event, population, period, and decision relevance.
- A revised forecast does not erase a miss or amend a binding commitment. A deliberately pessimistic forecast is not made accurate by later “over-delivery.”
